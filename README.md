VARSITYWARES | MERN STACK 

The idea of the project was to create a medium to sell the varsity wares (i.e tshirt, joursey, hoodie, cap etc.) among the specific university students. From any university students can register as seller whoever wants to sell and will upload their products. All the products will be shown university wise(Just for the comparison and competition for well products). But only respective university products can be bought.

Features

1.Register as a seller

2.Admin Panel for managing products and orders

3.Searching and filtering by queries

4.Best selling Products

5.AddCart to checkout(bkash payment integrated)

6.Coupon System

7.Real Time chat between buyer and seller and for reviews comment(socket)
